How to install

  1. On your computerOpen Tachan.exe: it installs itself. Allow it on your network and a QR code appears. If Windows warns you: More info, Run anyway.
  2. On your phonePoint your camera at the computer's QR. If you don't have the app, it downloads on its own. Android asks you to allow the install because it isn't on Google Play yet.
  3. Link themConfirm on your phone and you're done. Both must be on the same wifi.
