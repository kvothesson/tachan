How to copy text from your phone and paste it on your PC

A code, an address, a link or a long message: you have it on your phone and need it on your computer. These are the ways to move it, from the most common to the most direct.

1. Send it to yourself

By email, or in a chat with yourself in a messaging app. Then you open it on the computer and copy it. It always works, but it takes several steps, and it stays stored in those services.

2. With a notes app

Google Keep or any notes app that syncs. You paste it into a note on your phone and copy it from the website on your computer. Good for things you want to keep, less so for moving something quickly.

3. With Phone Link or KDE Connect

Phone Link, from Microsoft, shares the clipboard on some phones, such as Samsung ones, with your Microsoft account. KDE Connect is free and open source, and also shares the clipboard over your wifi.

4. With Tachán

This is the one we make. Select the text or link on your phone, tap Share and pick Tachán. A small notice appears on your computer without taking you away from what you were doing, and the text is already copied: Ctrl+V wherever you want. It goes over your wifi, encrypted, with no account and no servers.

  1. On your computer: download Tachan.exe and open it. It installs itself and shows a QR code.
  2. On your phone: point the camera at the QR code. If you don't have the app, it downloads on its own. Confirm and they're linked.
  3. To send: share the text with Tachán and paste it on the computer with Ctrl+V.

It also works for photos, videos and files. For now it's only for Android and Windows, and both must be on the same wifi.
